Ten-Step Skill Method

How One Sentence Becomes a Scene

Creative direction comes before templates: proportions, grounded physics or anatomy sources, interactive controls, and observed frame inspection.

01

Intent & Visual Direction

Establish silhouette, lighting mood, camera framing, and palette before writing geometry code.

02

Object Reasoning & Routing

Decompose parts, joints, and scales; route between proved blueprint kits, procedural rigs, or registered meshes.

03

Build & Grounded Behavior

Bind motion and cutaways to real underlying state while stating scope, sources, and honest limits.

04

Run, Capture & Refine

Inspect rendered viewpoints and interactions directly in WebGL, refining lighting and geometry from observed frames.
